Vice City Nights turns your own photos into GTA 6-style cover art that reads as designed key art, not a photo collage: four panel layouts (Poster, Triptych, a four-plate Quad grid, and a hero-plus-strip Banner), five colour grades including a posterised halftone look and a stronger warm sunset, a huge gradient caption with a slanted neon script line under it, and real poster furniture drawn by the app — a starburst badge, a tagline bar, a halftone band and a five-star row. Export as a PNG in square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350) or landscape (1920 × 1080) post sizes. It runs entirely offline in the browser — photos never leave your machine.

Pick a photo for each of the four panels and crop it in place with zoom and pan, or load the samples with varied crops in one click, or fill every panel with one photo. Type the big caption, the script line, the tagline and the badge words. Three steps from photo to PNG: load, caption, export.

## Screenshots

The four screenshots in `screenshots/` are all drawn by the app itself: the cover is the posterize Quad landscape, the editor shows the warm Banner layout with samples loaded, then a neon Poster portrait and a sunset Triptych square, each with strongly different crops so the two sample photos read as four different plates.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load a photo into each panel (or load the samples), type a caption, and export the PNG.
