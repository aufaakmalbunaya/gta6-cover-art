Vice City Nights turns your own photos into GTA 6-style cover art: a multi-panel poster with a slanted gutter layout, a synthwave sun, a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, and bold condensed caption lettering, exported as a PNG in square (1080 × 1080), portrait (1080 × 1350) or landscape (1920 × 1080) post sizes. It runs entirely offline in the browser — photos never leave your machine.

Pick a photo for each panel and crop it in place, or fill every panel with one photo. Three steps from photo to PNG: load, caption, export.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, pick a photo for each panel (or fill all panels with one), type a caption, and export the PNG.
